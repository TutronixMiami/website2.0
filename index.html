05 / FAQ

What families ask first.

Is the planning session separate from tutoring?+

Yes. It is a focused 30-minute session at the beginning of the week, designed to map the full academic workload before subject tutoring begins.

What happens during weekly planning?+

We review assignments, tests, projects, deadlines and commitments, then prioritize tasks, break down larger work and create a realistic plan.

Is Tutronix tutoring or executive function coaching?+

Both work together. Academic tutoring addresses content while the planning structure builds practical organization, time management and follow through skills.

Do you offer tutoring in person in Miami?+

Yes, depending on location and availability. Virtual sessions are also available.
