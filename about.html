WHY TUTRONIX EXISTS

Understanding changes the way you teach.

Andrew Senorans founded Tutronix because he understands what it feels like to be capable and still struggle with the systems that make school manageable.

The Tutronix Method grows from that lived experience: zoom out first, make the work visible, build a realistic plan, and then use tutoring with purpose. Students receive academic support while practicing the organization, prioritization and follow-through that lead to greater independence.
